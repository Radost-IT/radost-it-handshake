## TAM, SAM, SOM (our estimate)

Assumption: Handshake charges 0.5% to 1% of the money that goes through escrow, on top of normal PayPal processing. We found no public benchmark for this fee.

- **TAM, agent-orchestrated commerce worldwide in 2030:** $3T to $5T (McKinsey) × 0.5% to 1% = **$15B to $50B a year** in fees.
- **SAM, US purchases that agents complete in 2030:** $190B to $385B (Morgan Stanley; Bain's $300B to $500B is in the same range) × 0.5% to 1% = **$1B to $3.9B a year**.
- **SOM, our 2030 target:** 1% of the SAM's money, $1.9B to $3.85B, × 0.75% = **$14M to $29M a year**.

Say it this way: "Forecasts by McKinsey, Morgan Stanley and Bain; the fee and share are our assumptions."
